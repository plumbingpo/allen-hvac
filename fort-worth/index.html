When Your System Fails in Fort Worth

The emergency calls we answer tend to follow a pattern. On the cooling side, it's an AC blowing warm air, an outdoor unit that hums without starting, or an evaporator coil iced over. Two common situations call for quick action from you before a technician arrives. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: turn the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so warm indoor air thaws the coil, because continuing to run it can flood the drain pan and damage the compressor. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or produces a burning smell. Turn it off at the service switch and give us a call. Those few minutes can prevent a far bigger repair.

Keeping Your Fort Worth HVAC System Running Longer

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is simple maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep vegetation trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still inexpensive.

How old is your system? That number tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and also shows higher bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement when the first heat wave hits.

Small problems don't stay minor for long. Consider a weak capacitor: swapping it out is a short, affordable repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can burn out the compressor, and just like that a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to keep our number in their phone and call at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and prevents a few miserable nights.
